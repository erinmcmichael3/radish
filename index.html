See Behind the Magic

At the heart of Radish is a team that lives and breathes food, fun, and flawless service. Bursting with energy and a passion for all things culinary, our staff brings contagious enthusiasm to every event, committed to making your celebration feel effortless, joyful, and deliciously memorable.

Our Journey

With over 50 years in the catering industry, Radish has provided top-tier cuisine and flawless service to events nationwide. Rooted in the belief that ingredients should come directly from the earth, we partner with local farms in each area we serve to craft only the freshest offerings.

Radish brings more than just delicious food to your event—we bring a passion for exquisite cuisine, expert knowledge of the culinary craft, and creativity to every bite. We’ve re-imagined culinary classics giving each dish a fresh, effortless twist taking your event to the next level. Let us help you make your next moment unforgettable.
